<threat_model>
ASVS L1. Phase-level threats this plan mitigates:
- **Confidence-spoofing / data-integrity (core bug):** the new `tg_confidence`/`tg_resolved_by`/`tg_probe_state` columns are the persistence layer that lets the worker (Wave 3) refuse to finalize a suspect checker's `not_registered`. Without these columns there is no place to record provenance, so the columns + CHECK constraint (`tg_confidence IN ('high','low')`) are the integrity foundation. Mitigation lands here as schema; enforcement lands in Plan 03.
- **Self-inflicted DoS on resolution capacity (account safety):** the `CONTACT_CHECK_*` knobs are the tunable that caps resolve volume to stay under the ~45-50 shadow-ban onset. Defaults must be conservative (burst ≤30, pace ≥2s, daily ≤400) so a misconfigured deploy cannot uncap the worker. Mitigation: ship safe defaults; enforcement in Plan 02.
No new attack surface (no endpoints, no secrets, no PII writes). Migration is DDL-only and idempotent (fail-fast guarantees no half-applied state).
</threat_model>
