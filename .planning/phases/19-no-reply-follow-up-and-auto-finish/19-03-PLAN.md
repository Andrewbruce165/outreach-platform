<objective>
Implement the D-17 double guard and the D-03 revert. In the listener: when a genuine contact message arrives, revert a `no_reply` conversation to `active` (so the normal AI answerer fires) and cancel that conversation's pending ping queue items. In the queue: add a pre-send sibling guard that, for follow-up ping items only, skips+cancels the ping if the contact replied after the ping was scheduled or the conversation left active/no_reply.

Purpose: These are the safety mechanisms that make it acceptable to snapshot ping text at enqueue time (queue never re-runs the LLM). Without them, a ping scheduled hours ago could send into a dialog the contact already answered. This plan owns listener.py + queue.py exclusively (no overlap with Plan 19-02's ai_engine/schemas/campaigns files) so it runs in parallel with 19-02.
Output: listener revert+cancel hook, queue pre-send replied-since guard.
</objective>
