<objective>
Close the #1 risk: make warmup isolation DETERMINISTIC. Replace the cache/pool/phone-dependent warmup-skip with a per-workspace internal-sender `telegram_id` short-circuit, symmetric across the inbound and outbound handlers, dropping internal traffic before any DB write or AI dispatch. Turns the RED WARM-01/02/04 tests green.

Purpose: The historical pollution (5382 fake `sent`, AI triggered on our own 13 accounts) came from a phone-based / pool-membership-scoped filter that leaked. D-01/D-02 mandate a deterministic signal keyed on `telegram_id ∈ {senders of this workspace}`. WARM-15: this is also the documented root-cause fix vs the old telegram-api phone-only filter.
Output: a workspace-aware listener with a deterministic, test-proven internal short-circuit.
</objective>
