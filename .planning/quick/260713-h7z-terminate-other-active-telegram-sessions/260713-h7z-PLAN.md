<objective>
Terminate all OTHER active Telegram sessions on every imported account (sender and checker roles alike) immediately after `get_me()` succeeds during account import, so a vendor who kept their own Telegram Desktop logged in with the same auth_key from a different IP can no longer trigger Telegram's concurrent-use auth_key kill.

Purpose: vendor tdata/session sellers frequently stay logged into the account they sold; when both our listener and their client use the same auth_key from different IPs, Telegram revokes the key and the imported account silently dies. Resetting other authorizations at import time claims exclusive ownership of the session.

Output: a best-effort `auth.ResetAuthorizations` call wired into `import_one_account`, plus unit tests proving it runs on success and that its failure cannot break the import.
</objective>
