# Quick Task 260622-j52 — Requeue 37 antispam auto-cancelled contacts

## Problem

Campaign `b7cc7d06-aece-42b1-9c56-b34f50789abd` (status `running`) had **0 pending**:
37 contacts stuck in `failed` with `Auto-cancelled: antispam signal received from …`,
never attempted (`attempts=NULL`). All 37 were cancelled in a **single bulk event** at
`2026-06-19 13:07:55 UTC` — the signature of `listener._handle_antispam_signal` firing once
and flipping every pending/processing queue row of the sender to `failed`.

Account is now healthy (`restriction_status=none`, `restricted_until=NULL`, `auth_status=ok`,
`lifecycle_status=active`). Pause→start does not help: enqueue dedupes and `failed` is terminal.

A requeue does **not** re-trigger the auto-cancel — that only fires on a *new* inbound antispam
signal, and the recent SpamBot self-check guard (260622-gxt) prevents solicited replies from
tripping it.

## Scope (by `error_message`)

| Bucket | Count | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Auto-cancelled%` (antispam) | 37 | **requeue** |
| PEER_FLOOD (`Спам-ограничение…`, attempts=3) | 1 | leave (optional separate retry; including it would make pending=38) |
| PRIVACY_PREMIUM_REQUIRED (attempts=3) | 1 | exclude — genuinely unreachable |

## Tasks

1. **Backup** — `./backup.sh` (manual pg_dump, ~1s).
2. **Requeue** the 37, idempotent by `campaign_id` + `error_message LIKE 'Auto-cancelled%'`,
   inside a transaction with a pre-mutation count guard:
   ```sql
   UPDATE message_queue
   SET status='pending', scheduled_at=NOW(), attempts=0,
       error_message=NULL, started_at=NULL, finished_at=NULL
   WHERE campaign_id='b7cc7d06-aece-42b1-9c56-b34f50789abd'
     AND status='failed' AND error_message LIKE 'Auto-cancelled%';
   ```
3. **Verify** `pending=37`, then observe the worker (api logs): `sent` grows, rate ≤ 4/min,
   no new flood/antispam.
