<objective>
Encode the empirically-verified semantics of the phone checker into documentation so future
developers do not misread `contacts_cache.is_registered=false` as "this number has no Telegram
account". The real meaning is weaker: "not resolvable by phone by a stranger (checker) account" —
which is also what happens when the owner sets "Who can find me by my phone number" to
Contacts/Nobody. The not_registered bucket therefore contains an unknown share of registered-but-
private numbers (false negatives).

Purpose: Prevent a class of future analytics/feature bugs (e.g. treating not_registered as "dead
number", building dedup on it, or renaming the field without understanding the caveat). The
verified evidence: the same healthy checker (`sender-8428118140`, 83 numbers resolved
is_registered=true, most recent the prior day) threw PhoneNotOccupiedError on our OWN authorized
sender accounts' phone numbers because they have restrictive find-by-phone privacy.

Output: Updated docstring + inline comment in `app/services/checker.py`, and a short Russian
caveat note in the project `CLAUDE.md`.

Scope guard: DOCUMENTATION/COMMENTS ONLY. Zero behavioral change. Explicitly NOT in scope —
renaming the column to `resolvable_by_phone`, adding a status enum, any migration, any change to
checker logic, any change to `contact_check_worker.py`.
</objective>

<facts_to_encode>
1. ResolvePhoneRequest raising PhoneNotOccupiedError (and ImportContactsRequest returning empty)
   does NOT prove the number has no Telegram account. It means the checker — a stranger account —
   cannot discover the number by phone. Also happens when the owner sets "Who can find me by my
   phone number" to Contacts/Nobody.
2. Proof it is privacy, not a broken checker: the same checker threw PhoneNotOccupiedError on our
   OWN live, authorized sender accounts' numbers (restrictive privacy), while having resolved 83
   other numbers as is_registered=true (most recent the prior day) — so it is healthy.
3. Therefore is_registered=false means "not resolvable by phone by a stranger account", NOT
   "definitely no Telegram account". The not_registered bucket contains an unknown share of
   registered-but-private numbers (false negatives). Operationally acceptable for cold phone-import
   outreach (you can't DM them by phone anyway), but the field NAME is misleading.
4. The only API way to confirm a privacy-hidden account exists is via its @username
   (ResolveUsernameRequest) — which check_usernames already does.
</facts_to_encode>
</context>
